CLEAN NOTES - USER MANUAL
=========================
Clean Notes, version 1.0.0+1. Android 5.0 or newer. July 2026.

Clean Notes is a simple notes app. Notes are saved to the cloud and
follow your subscription account across reinstalls and new phones.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Android 5.0 or newer.
- A Bangladeshi mobile SIM that can receive SMS.
- Internet access (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
- BDT 2/= per day, added to your phone bill by your operator through
  AppsPro / BDApps. That is about BDT 60/= per 30-day month.

1. INSTALL
- Open the APK file on your phone.
- Allow installs from this source if Android asks.
- Tap Install, then Open.

2. SUBSCRIBE (BDT 2/= PER DAY)
The first time you open the app you must subscribe. You cannot use
the app until you do.

1. Type your Bangladeshi mobile number in the format 8801XXXXXXXXX.
2. Tap "Send code".
3. Wait for the OTP by SMS (usually under 30 seconds).
4. Type the OTP and tap "Verify".
5. Tap "Continue to notes" on the confirmation screen.

The BDT 2/= daily charge starts the day you finish step 4. It stops
the day after you unsubscribe (see section 4). You will see it on
your operator's monthly statement under value-added services.

If the OTP does not arrive: wait two minutes, check your signal,
make sure your plan does not block SMS from short codes, then tap
"Resend code". If it still does not arrive, restart your phone and
try again.

3. USING THE APP
- Notes list: the main screen. Most recently edited notes first.
- Add a note: tap the "+" button, type, then tap the back arrow.
  Your note saves automatically.
- Edit a note: tap the note in the list, change the text, tap the
  back arrow. The "last edited" time updates.
- Delete a note: open the note, tap the trash-can icon, confirm.
  This cannot be undone.

4. UNSUBSCRIBE AND LOG OUT
Tap "Logout / Unsubscribe" at the bottom of the notes list. The app
calls the operator to cancel your subscription, then removes your
notes from this device. The next time you open the app you will be
asked to subscribe again.

It may take up to 24 hours for the unsubscribe to fully reflect on
your operator bill. If the BDT 2/= daily charge still appears after
24 hours, contact your mobile operator.

5. NEW PHONE
Install Clean Notes on the new phone and subscribe with the same
Bangladeshi mobile number. Your notes will load from the cloud.
A different phone number sees an empty list.

6. OFFLINE
You can read notes that were previously loaded on this device
without internet. New notes save locally and sync the next time
you are online. Subscribing for the first time needs internet.

7. PRIVACY
- Notes are stored in Cloud Firestore under your subscriber
  identity. No other user can see them.
- Logging out removes your notes from this device. The cloud copy
  stays linked to your subscriber account.
- The app does not collect your contacts, location, camera, or
  microphone.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING
- "Continue to notes" does nothing: fixed in version 1.0.0+1.
- OTP never arrives: see section 2.
- Unsubscribe returns an error: the app still logs you out locally.
  Try again later or contact your operator.
- App crashes on startup: free 50 MB of storage, restart the phone,
  or uninstall and reinstall.
- My notes are gone: are you on the same phone number? Did you open
  the app offline so new notes never synced? If yes to both, try
  uninstalling and reinstalling.

9. SUPPORT
- Billing or the BDT 2/= daily charge: contact your mobile operator
  and ask about "AppsPro" or "BDApps" on your account.
- App issues: see APPSPRO_INTEGRATION.md in the project repository,
  or contact the team that gave you the app.
